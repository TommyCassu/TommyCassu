
### Software & Web Developer in training 42 School 
___

🎯 **Actively looking for my first internship / junior developer role**

I'm a student at **42 School** — a project-based program with no classes and no professors, where you learn by shipping working code, breaking it, and fixing it yourself. It's intense, but it forces you to actually understand what you're building instead of following a lesson plan. Before that, I studied at **BTS SIO**, which is where I first got hands-on with development.

42's early curriculum throws you straight into **C and C++**, which pushed me to understand what's really happening under the hood before ever reaching for a framework. But my real goal is to build **web and software products that people actually use day to day**, so I've been steadily moving from systems-level projects into full web development.

Alongside school, I also work a student job in a hypermarket — it's given me a first real taste of the work world outside a classroom or a screen. Dealing with customers, handling rush hours, and working as part of a team taught me things 42 doesn't: how to stay reliable under pressure, communicate clearly with people who aren't technical, and just show up and do the work even on the days it's not exciting. It's not a coding job, but it's shaped how I approach responsibility — and I think that matters as much as the technical side when starting a career.
